# Play Console checklist — Marion Wx Map (donation-only) DRAFT

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Do not invent fees, review times, or policy interpretations. Follow what Google’s pages show at signup time.

## 1. Create a Play Console account

1. Go to https://play.google.com/console/signup  
2. Sign in with the Google account you want as the **publisher** account.  
3. Accept the terms Google shows.  
4. Pay Google’s **one-time registration fee** — use the **amount shown on Google’s page** (do not rely on a remembered dollar figure).  
5. Complete identity / verification steps as Console asks.

## 2. Create the app (free / donation-only)

1. In Play Console: **Create app**.  
2. Set the app as **Free** (not a paid download).  
3. Do **not** set up Google Play Billing for tips. Tips are optional Cash App support only.  
4. Put the Cash App tip ($AnthonyDean16) in the **store listing** and optionally in-app as an **external** tip link.  
5. Follow **current** Google Play payments / monetization policy for external tips/donations yourself — this checklist is not a compliance determination.
