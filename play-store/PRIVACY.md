## Summary

Marion Wx Map does not require an account. The publisher does not operate a backend that collects personal data for the map itself. The app fetches public weather, lightning, and ADS-B data from third-party services over the network from your device.

## Information the app uses

### Map and weather data (on your device)

When you use the map, your device requests publicly available data (for example weather radar, storm-related layers, lightning, ADS-B aircraft positions, and map tiles) from third-party providers over the internet. Those requests go from your device to those providers. How those providers handle request metadata (such as IP address) is governed by their own policies.

The publisher of Marion Wx Map does **not** collect that personal data on a publisher server for the map product itself.
